    How your data stays put

    Why observational studies fail so quietly

    Most of what goes wrong goes wrong before any model is fit. None of it shows up as an error message.

    A start date that drifts

    Follow-up begins at a different moment for different patients, and the gap quietly becomes time in which nobody could have had the outcome.

    Eligibility from the future

    A criterion is judged using information recorded after assignment, so the cohort is selected by something that had not happened yet.

    A comparison no trial could make

    Strategies defined by what happened later, or groups that could never have been randomized, produce an answer to a question nobody asked.

    How it started

    TriEmulate grew out of doctoral research in epidemiology on target trial emulation for clinical decision making. Before that came clinical practice, a public health degree, and five years as lead analyst on federally funded public health research. The same mistakes kept appearing in the design stage, long before any model was fit, so the design stage is what the engine automates.

    What we believe

    A design that visibly fails is better than one that quietly looks right. Every choice should carry its reason, every gap should be named, and the judgments that belong to the investigator should stay with the investigator. Good research tools should make honest work easier, not make weak work look finished.

    Questions before you book a demo?

    Does TriEmulate run the analysis or produce effect estimates?
    No. It writes the design: the protocol, the causal graph, the schema mapping and the reporting structure. It names the quantity being estimated and what the analysis must account for, and the choice of estimator stays with your analysts. It never produces a figure it did not read from your data.
    Does it need access to patient records?
    No. Mapping runs against a catalog of your schema alone: table names, column names and data types. Uploaded schema descriptions are admitted only if they carry schema metadata and no record-level content, so the check can run where the records themselves cannot be touched.
    Which data models does it support?
    A catalog for OMOP CDM v5.4 is built in, covering 39 tables and 432 columns. Any other source is supported by uploading a description of its schema. The design layer itself is independent of any one database.
    What happens when a variable is not in my data?
    It is reported as unresolved. The engine does not substitute the nearest available field, and it does not quietly repair its own output into something that reads as complete.
    Can I disagree with the choices it makes?
    That is the point of the provenance record. Every stage states what it decided and why, including the alternatives it rejected, so a reviewer can challenge one specific choice rather than the design as a whole.
    Is TriEmulate available now?
    It is under active development and not yet generally available, but it runs today. We can walk through it end to end on a question of your own. A United States provisional patent application covering the system was filed in September 2026.
